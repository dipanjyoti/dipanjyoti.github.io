    <p>
      <strong>Dr. Dipanjyoti Paul</strong> is an Assistant Professor in the
      Department of Artificial Intelligence
      at the Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) Kharagpur, India.
      His research focuses on developing interpretable and reliable artificial
      intelligence methods for computer vision, multimodal learning,
      ecology & biodiversity research, and healthcare. Prior to joining IIT Kharagpur, he served as an Assistant Professor in the Department of Information Technology at the Indian Institute of Engineering Science and Technology (IIEST) Shibpur, India, and as an Assistant Professor in the Department of Computer Science at the University of Tsukuba, Japan. He has also been associated with research groups, Imageomics Institute at The Ohio State University, USA, and the Center for Artificial Intelligence Research (C-AIR) at the University of Tsukuba, Japan. He received his Ph.D. from the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at the Indian Institute of Technology Patna, India. His research has been published in leading computer vision and artificial intelligence venues, including CVPR and ICLR, as well as journals such as Pattern Recognition, Knowledge-Based Systems, and Expert Systems with Applications. His work has also appeared in several ACM and IEEE Transactions journals.
    </p>
