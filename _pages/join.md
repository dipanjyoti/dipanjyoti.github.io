## Join My Research Group

I am looking for **highly motivated PhD students** to join my research group in the areas of **Computer Vision and Acoustic Signal Processing for Ecology and Biodiversity, and Medical Imaging.**. I am also happy to hear from **Master's and Undergraduate students** who are interested in research and would like to work with me. 

If you are interested, please feel free to email me your **CV**, along with a brief summary of your research skills, relevant research experience, and your **two most significant publications**, highlighting your contributions to each.
