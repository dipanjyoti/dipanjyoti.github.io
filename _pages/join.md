## Join My Research Group

I am looking for **highly motivated PhD students** to join my research group in the areas of **Intersection of Machine Learning and Biology particularly utilizing Computer Vision for Ecology and Biodiversity, Acoustic Signal Processing and Medical Imaging**.

I am also happy to hear from **Master's and undergraduate students** who are interested in research and would like to work with me. If you are interested, please feel free to email me your **CV** along with a brief description of your research interests and relevant experience.

### What I Look For

Students working with me are expected to have:

- A good understanding of **Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning fundamentals**
- Strong **programming and coding skills**
- Enthusiasm for **learning new concepts and exploring new ideas**
- A strong interest in **research, experimentation, and problem-solving**
- The motivation to work independently while collaborating with others

You do not need to know everything beforehand. **Curiosity, willingness to learn, and genuine interest in research are equally important.**

### Research & Collaboration

Members of my research group will have opportunities to work on research problems in **AI, Machine Learning, Computer Vision, Multimodal AI, and Explainable AI**, including applications in areas such as **biology, biodiversity, and medical imaging**.

There will also be opportunities to collaborate with **computer scientists and medical experts** from leading universities and research institutions, including:

- **University of Tsukuba, Japan**
- **The Ohio State University, USA**
- **Purdue University, USA**
- **University of Washington, USA**

### International Opportunities

There may also be opportunities for **international research internships in Japan** through the [**Sakura Science Exchange Program**](https://ssp.jst.go.jp/en/) for **undergraduate, master's, and PhD students**.

These opportunities typically involve research visits of approximately **3–6 months** and provide students with exposure to international research environments and collaborations.

### Interested in Joining?

If you are interested in working with me, please send an email with:

- Your **CV**
- A brief description of your **research interests**
- Your **relevant academic/research experience**
- Your **programming and technical skills**
- Your preferred **area of research**, if you have one

I encourage students who are genuinely interested in **AI/ML research** to get in touch. I look forward to hearing from you!
