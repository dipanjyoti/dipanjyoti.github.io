About

Dr. Dipanjyoti Paul is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Artificial Intelligence at the Indian Institute of Technology Kharagpur. He is a member of the Center for Artificial Intelligence Research (C-AIR) at the University of Tsukuba, Japan and the Imageomics Institute at The Ohio State University, USA.

Prior to joining IIT Kharagpur, he served as an Assistant Professor at the Indian Institute of Engineering Science & Technology (IIEST), Shibpur, India, and the University of Tsukuba, Japan. He also worked as a postdoctoral fellow in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at The Ohio State University, USA.

His research interests lie at the intersection of machine learning and biology, particularly in computer vision and acoustic signal processing for ecology and biodiversity, explainable AI, and medical imaging.
